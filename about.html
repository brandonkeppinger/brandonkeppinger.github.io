About me

I’m an Honors Mathematics student at the University of Notre Dame with interests in computer science, artificial intelligence, physics, and computational problem solving.

I’m especially interested in problems where mathematical and physical ideas can be turned into working systems: implementing machine learning algorithms, understanding how intelligent systems learn and reason, and exploring applications in areas such as robotics. I enjoy learning ideas from first principles and then testing that understanding by building things myself.

Mathematics remains an important part of how I think, particularly through my background in topology, analysis, algebra, and number theory. I’m increasingly drawn to questions at the intersection of mathematics, physics, computation, and experimentation, where theory can be connected to systems that behave in the real world.

Interests

  • Machine learning and artificial intelligence
  • Physics and physical systems
  • Robotics and intelligent systems
  • Algorithms, computation, and software
  • Mathematics and mathematical problem solving
